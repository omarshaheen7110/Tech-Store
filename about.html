Our Story

TECH STORE started in 2015 as a small shop in Nasr City selling cases and chargers. Over the years we grew into a full store carrying flagship smartphones and accessories, and in 2019 we added a same-day repair center with certified technicians. Today, with over 40,000 customers served, our promise remains the same: honest advice, fair prices, and a great experience every time.
